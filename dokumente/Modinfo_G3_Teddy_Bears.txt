GGG Teddy Bears

This mod will bring more teddy bears to the game, and offers the following:

28 New Teddy Bears (16 Storage)
1 New Belt Item( GGG Teddy Tote, has 36 storage and holds 3 bears)
1 Teddy Bookcase kit to use as a base storage item(holds 12) and displays them in your base!
1 set of 3D Glasses also included

Types and classnames are included

Notes:
Vanilla bears do not work in these
When disassembling bookcase, remove teddys first.
Bookcase kit needs to be spun 180degrees before placing kit
