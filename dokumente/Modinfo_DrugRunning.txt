# iM7s_Drug Running Mod

**iM7s_DrugRunning** expands upon some of the existing aspects of the game’s cultivation and crafting mechanics while introducing new items, ingredients, recipes, workstations, and the ability to manufacture ‘illicit’ products to enhance server economy.

This mod also leverages the framework of [IAT_Crafting_Plus](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3142819902) and [IAT_Crafting_Plus_Enums](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3142820195) to accommodate the ability to craft complex recipes on workstations. 

- As of update `version 1.01` — these are required dependencies to run this mod.

# Cultivation

iM7s_DrugRunning aims to extend upon the cultivation mechanics for it’s various horticulture aspects. It does so by introducing new features such indoor grow operation abilities (i.e. Grow Tents & Grow Pots) and new methods of cultivation for certain products (i.e. psilocybin mushrooms).

This page will cover the topic of all the mod’s plants and cultivation related items that can be utilized in the process.

### **Cultivation Equipment:**

### **Grow Tents:**

Bringing indoor grow operations to life in the apocalypse.

Grow Tents allow for easy indoor setup to accommodate a single plant within it’s confines.

To get started:

- Acquire a **Grow Tent Kit**
- Deploy the kit in your desired location.
- Plant your desired crop into it’s attachment slot
- Water & let it grow.
- More will be added as we expand upon this feature.

### **Grow Pots:**

- Acquire a **Grow Pot Kit**
- Deploy the kit in your desired location.
- Plant your desired crop into it’s attachment slot
- Water & let it grow.

Check back for more information once this feature is released.

### **Mushroom Cultivation:**

Psilocybin cultivation has never been more immersive in DayZ. Let’s dive into how it works:

- In order to get started, you will need to acquire a **Spore Syringe.** They come in **5** variants:
    - Blue Veil Growtime: 2400 seconds
    - Ghost CapsGrowtime: 3000 seconds
    - SunsporesGrowtime: 3600 seconds
    - Golden TeacherGrowtime: 4200 seconds
    - Mystic VoidGrowtime: 4800 seconds
- Once you have a **Spore Syringe**, you can now approach any **Garden Plot** with in in-hand and **Inject** the **Spore Culture** into the **Soil**/**Substrate** to start the process.
- Add **Water** to the filled seed slot.
- Protect your colony crops!
    - The growth process is default set to 1 Hour to full maturity. However, you will be able to harvest once it reaches harvestable stages of growth.
	
	
	# Cannabis Recipes

The wholesome realm of the cannabis cultivation trade.

This pursuit is quite commonly taken up by a vast array of survivors looking to further their ability to earn a decent income to sustain a more than satisfactory quality of life.

But it’s not always an easy one and humble one.

Crops are often discovered by those who prowl on opportunity to come up quick without doing the tedious tending of plants throughout their growth cycles.

For those who dive into the cannabis trade, or those who find themselves in possession of it’s bounties, the recipes for crafting is quite basic.

**Cannabis** products currently have **three** available **quantity** types:

- **Buds** (chunky 1/2 oz. beauties)
- **Zips** (ounces)
- **Bricks**

**All recipes require the same strain of buds in order to craft.**

**Crafting Zips:**

- 1 x **Bud** + 1 x **Bud** = 1 x **Zip**
- 1 x **Zip** + any vanilla knife = 2 x **Buds**

**Crafting Bricks:**

Attaching full stacks of **Buds** from one strain to each available slot on the **Production Table** under the **Cannabis Products** panel and approaching the table with **Duct Tape** or **Ziplock Bags** in hand will bring up the option to **Craft a Brick**.

- Requirements:
    - Production Table
    - Duct Tape or Ziplock Bag
    - 16 Buds total (same strain)

# Cocaine Recipes

The cocaine manufacturing process is quite simple.

The process begins with cultivation of **Coca Seeds;** harvesting leaves from the ****mature **Coca Bush;** and combining the **leaves** with **Acetone** to create **Coke Pile**(s); and combining the **Coke Piles** into **Cocaine Bundles.**

- This stage of production can be accomplished via regular crafting (combining the two items in hand + vicinity)

The production of larger **bulk quantity** items **(Bricks)** takes place through interaction with a **Production Table** for complex crafting integrations to package the **Bundles** into a **Brick**.

- For more information on this part of the production process:

# Cocaine Bricks

Cocaine Bricks

In order to start cashing in on the cocaine trade market at a significant lucrative level, you will need to start producing large quantity in bulk.

In order to accomplish this, you will need to have some things in order first:

- Production Table
- 5 x Cocaine Bundles
- Duct Tape or Ziplock Bags

# High End Recipes

One of the “high-end” lucrative options, of course, is transportation of the most valuable amounts of ‘weight’ and product(s) to the Trader as possible.

However, this route, often does not come easy and will require players to invest their grind into a combination of acquiring the **required ingredients**, **amounts of product**, and **other production steps** involved in order to produce top-tier level quantities to gain the reward of top-dollar payouts for their efforts.

Not only is this a risky process, vulnerable and susceptible to banditry and other threats, but it can get a bit complex. I believe that this only contributes more so to the overall immersion that is aimed at being provided by this expansive mod.

With that being said — let’s get into the **complexities** of ‘**Brick’ Crafting** recipes.

You’ll be needing some solid workspaces to produce any amounts of real weight.

To get started, you’ll need to grab some common materials, or obtain a pre-made kit for the following workbenches:

## **WORKSTATION RECIPES:**

**Lab Station Kit:** *(required for production of chemical based products)**

- **2x Sheet Metal** + **Metal Pipe** = **Lab Station Kit**
- **Screwdriver** + **Lab Station** = Dismantle to Kit

**Production Table Kit:** *(required for bagging up bricks of any given product)**

- **2x Sheet Metal** + **Metal Pipe** = **Production Table Kit**
- Screwdriver + Production Table = Dismantle to Kit

## **PRODUCT RECIPES:**

Once you have that sorted out and set up, you’re ready for the next steps. Depending on which line of ‘work’ you pursue, (i.e. cannabis, methamphetamine, cocaine, ecstasy, etc.) you’ll need to make sure you have the right amount & types of products to elevate into the big leagues.

# Cannabis Bricks

Cannabis Bricks can be crafted at the Production Table by approaching it and attaching a total of 16 buds of the same strain to their respective slots.

# Meth Bricks

Once you’ve taken the necessary steps to manufacture your product using the Lab Station and necessary ingredients to produce a batch, you’re ready to break down those trays and gather up your shards onto the Production Table.

Attach 5 stacks of 20 (must be same batch type) to the respective slots located under the Meth Products tab.

Approach the Table with Duct Tape or Ziplock Bags in hand, and the option to craft Brick of Meth will appear.
